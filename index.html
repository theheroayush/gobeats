Privacy Policy for gobeats

Package Name: com.gobeats.player
Effective Date: October 11, 2026
Last Updated: October 11, 2026

🛡️ Zero-Tracking & 100% On-Device Guarantee

gobeats is designed as a standalone, offline-first media player. We do not require accounts, we do not show advertisements, we do not track your behavior, and we never collect or transmit your media library to any server.

1. Information We Access & Permissions

A. Media Storage Access (Local Audio & Video)

Permissions: READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (for Android 12 and below).

Used solely to scan, index, organize, and play your music and video files on your physical device. All song metadata and artwork cache are stored locally in your device's private SQLite database.

B. Background Audio & Screen-Off Playback

Permissions: FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK.

Allows high-fidelity audio to play continuously when the app is in the background or when your phone screen is turned off. A standard Android system media notification provides playback controls.

C. Local Wi-Fi PC Sync (PIN-Based File Transfer)

Permissions: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE.

Enables wireless music file transfer between your PC browser and your phone using a secure 6-digit PIN over your private local Wi-Fi network. No data or files ever travel through external cloud servers.

D. Offline AI Subtitles (Speech Recognition)

Technology: Android on-device SpeechRecognizer.

Generates dual subtitles entirely offline using native Google voice language models installed on your phone (supporting Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, and English). Audio is never uploaded or recorded.

E. Audio Output Device Profiles

Technology: Android AudioManager.

Detects when you connect Earbuds, Headphones, Car Audio, or Phone Speakers to automatically apply your preferred Equalizer presets and Bluetooth AV lip-sync delay compensations.

F. Synced Lyrics (LRCLIB)

When you play a track, gobeats queries public lyrics databases (LRCLIB) to fetch time-synchronized lyrics using only track title and artist name. No device identifiers, user accounts, or telemetry data are shared.

2. Data Retention & Deletion

All app-generated data (playlists, listening stats, equalizer presets, cached lyrics) resides purely on your device. You can permanently delete all data at any time by clearing the application storage via Settings > Apps > gobeats > Storage > Clear Data or by uninstalling the application.

3. Children's Privacy

gobeats does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. The application complies fully with COPPA and GDPR regulations.
